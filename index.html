Current research

Anchor-Currency Volatility and Macroeconomic Transmission under a Peg

This project, developed through an independent study with Prof. Aaron Smallwood, examines volatility transmission under Nepal’s fixed exchange-rate arrangement with India. The analysis uses econometric and time-series methods to study how external volatility relates to domestic macroeconomic outcomes.

Independent study with Prof. Aaron Smallwood · Econometrics · Time series

Texas Electricity Generation Forecasting

Built and validated seasonal forecasting models in R using unit-root testing, BIC and AICc model selection, STL decomposition, Box-Cox transformation, and residual diagnostics. The final SARIMA specification achieved 2.8% MAPE on a 12-month holdout sample.

R · SARIMA · Forecasting · EIA data · 2.8% MAPE
