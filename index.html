Current research

Anchor-Currency Volatility and Macroeconomic Transmission under a Peg

Current research on how volatility in an anchor currency can transmit through a small economy operating under a fixed exchange-rate regime, using Nepal’s peg with India as the empirical setting. The work uses a range of econometric and time-series methods to study macroeconomic transmission.

International economics · Monetary policy · Time series · Econometrics
