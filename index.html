Current research

Anchor-Currency Volatility and Macroeconomic Transmission under a Peg

This research examines how volatility in an anchor currency transmits to a small economy operating under a fixed exchange-rate regime, using Nepal's peg with India as the empirical setting. The analysis studies responses in inflation, credit, reserves, and the external balance using local projections, structural-change tests, asymmetric volatility models, and Bayesian stochastic-volatility methods.

International macroeconomics · Monetary policy · Time series · Econometrics

Quantitative economics and reproducible analysis

GDP, Capital Stock & Economic Growth — University of Utah

A broader GDP modeling project developed at the University of Utah that combined capital stock, human capital, productivity, population, and related international datasets. The project used ALITE for data integration and compared conventional econometric specifications with machine-learning approaches.

I later extended this line of work with additional econometric modeling and replication exercises, using the project as a base for comparing empirical specifications rather than treating prediction alone as the objective.

ALITE · Econometrics · Data integration · Machine learning · Python

Capital Stock and Growth in South Asia

An earlier cross-country analysis of how private capital, public capital, and the working population relate to GDP in major South Asian economies. The project compares log-linear specifications motivated by the Cobb–Douglas production function with machine-learning models and uses SHAP values to examine relative contributions.
